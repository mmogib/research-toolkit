# AIMS Press journals (for example AIMS Mathematics)

Public form labels and procedure only. At setup, confirm each point against the form the user has
in front of them; the current form takes precedence, and any difference goes in the brief. Nothing
from a particular submission belongs in this file.

## How the review arrives

- The invitation and acceptance emails come from the press's no-reply address. The acceptance
  email gives the manuscript ID, the type of manuscript, the title, and a link to the review form.
- **The form link is personal:** it opens the referee's review. Leave it in the email where it
  arrived; never copy it into notes, deliverables, or anything shared.
- The manuscript arrives as a PDF in the journal's template. Figures may arrive separately, for
  example as an archive.

## The review form

| Field | Kind | Prepared as |
|---|---|---|
| Keywords | the referee's own research keywords | outside the review |
| Comments and Suggestions for Authors | a text box plus a file upload ("Choose File") | the authors' report, compiled to PDF and uploaded; a short summary pasted in the box |
| Comments for Editors | a text box, labelled as not revealed to the authors | the editor comments, paste-ready |
| English Language and Style | one of four options | a form choice, from Track H |
| Overall Recommendation | one of four options | a form choice |
| Does this manuscript match the journal scope? | Yes / No | a form choice |

The options, as the form labels them:
- **English Language and Style:** "Extensive editing of English language and style required";
  "Moderate English changes required"; "English language and style are fine/minor spell check
  required"; "I don't feel qualified to judge about the English Language and Style".
- **Overall Recommendation:** "Accept in present form"; "Accept after minor revision";
  "Reconsider after major revision"; "Reject".
- **Scope:** "Yes"; "No".

## Points to settle with the user

- The form records the recommendation separately. Whether the authors' report also states it is
  the user's decision; record it in the brief.
- The comments for the editor are pasted as plain text: no LaTeX, and mathematics in plain
  notation.
- Leftover placeholders from the journal's template (for example an empty DOI or an instruction
  paragraph) are presentation items for the typos list.
